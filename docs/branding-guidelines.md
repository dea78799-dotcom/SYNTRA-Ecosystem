# SYNTRA Branding Guidelines

**Document version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Initial brand standard  
**Applies to:** SYNTRA Group, SYNTRA OS, Syntra Files, and future ecosystem products

This document defines the baseline rules for using the SYNTRA identity consistently. It records approved direction where known and marks visual specifications that still require confirmation. It does not replace the official source artwork.

## 1. Brand foundation

SYNTRA is the shared identity for a growing software ecosystem. Each product may have its own recognizable product mark and interface style, while remaining clearly connected to the parent brand.

Brand principles:

- **Clarity:** use simple, readable layouts and recognizable symbols.
- **Consistency:** keep shared identity elements stable across products.
- **Practicality:** prioritize legibility and usability over decoration.
- **Trust:** do not distort official marks or make unsupported product claims.
- **Accessibility:** maintain readable contrast, text sizes, and interaction states.

## 2. Official company logo

The official company logo is the artwork supplied by the project owner:

[Official SYNTRA logo source](https://github.com/dea78799-dotcom/Hinh-anh/blob/main/file_0000000076408209a2b7b2c33b416b28.png)

Usage rules:

- Use the supplied artwork as the source of truth.
- Do not redraw, recolor, stretch, skew, crop, or add effects to the logo without approval.
- Preserve its original aspect ratio and provide clear space around it.
- Use a background that keeps the logo legible; do not place it over visually busy content.
- Do not replace the official logo with generated or improvised artwork.
- If a different size or file format is needed, derive it from the original asset without changing the design.

The repository may link to the source artwork. A local copy should be added only when the project owner has selected and approved its exact file.

## 3. Product identity

### SYNTRA OS

SYNTRA OS uses the official SYNTRA identity as its parent-brand connection. Its product mark, interface color tokens, typography, and icon system must be confirmed against approved product artwork and actual interface decisions before being treated as final.

Do not invent a separate official SYNTRA OS logo or claim that provisional UI colors are approved brand colors.

### Syntra Files

The selected Syntra Files logo direction is:

- A classic, minimalist folder shape.
- A cream/beige folder surface.
- A dark serif letter **S**.
- A charcoal background.

Keep this direction restrained and legible. Do not substitute a generic brightly colored folder icon for the selected product identity without approval.

### Future products

A new product should use a distinct product name and, where needed, a product mark that remains visually compatible with the SYNTRA parent identity. Obtain approval before announcing a new mark as official.

## 4. Color system

The official company palette has not yet been documented with verified color values. Therefore:

- Do not publish guessed HEX, RGB, or CMYK values as official SYNTRA colors.
- Sample colors from the approved source artwork and obtain project-owner approval before adding permanent brand tokens.
- Record approved values, intended usage, and light/dark background behavior in a later revision of this document.
- Product-specific interface colors may be used during development, but must be labeled provisional until approved.

For Syntra Files, cream/beige and charcoal describe the selected logo direction; exact color codes remain to be confirmed.

## 5. Typography

- Prefer readable system or open-licensed fonts suitable for the target platform.
- Use a consistent type scale for titles, body text, labels, and supporting information.
- Maintain adequate line spacing and avoid overly decorative fonts in functional interfaces.
- The serif **S** is part of the selected Syntra Files mark; it does not establish a requirement to use serif typography throughout the application.
- Confirm font licensing before bundling a font with a product.

Specific font families and weights remain subject to product-level approval.

## 6. Layout and interface consistency

Across SYNTRA products:

- Use consistent spacing, alignment, and component behavior.
- Keep navigation labels understandable and concise.
- Make important actions visually distinguishable.
- Provide clear loading, empty, success, warning, and error states.
- Ensure touch targets are usable on mobile devices where mobile support is intended.
- Support different screen sizes without clipping essential content.
- Avoid decorative elements that reduce readability or obscure content.

Shared patterns should be documented in the relevant product design system before being treated as ecosystem-wide components.

## 7. Icons and illustrations

- Use a consistent icon style within each product.
- Prefer recognizable symbols with clear meaning.
- Do not use third-party icons or illustrations without checking their license and attribution requirements.
- Do not alter the official company logo to create feature icons.
- File-type icons in Syntra Files should communicate the file format accurately and remain visually consistent.

## 8. Image and media usage

- Use assets that the project owns or is authorized to use.
- Preserve the intended aspect ratio unless a documented crop is approved.
- Avoid low-resolution, watermarked, or misleading imagery.
- Optimize media for the target device without making text or symbols unclear.
- Do not include personal information, private paths, or sensitive content in screenshots used for public documentation.

## 9. Naming and brand writing

- Write the company name as **SYNTRA**.
- Write the operating-system product as **SYNTRA OS**.
- Write the file-management product as **Syntra Files**.
- Keep product names consistent in interfaces, documentation, repositories, and release notes.
- Do not describe a product as stable, released, certified, or fully secure unless that status has been verified and approved.

## 10. Do not

- Replace the official company logo with an unapproved redesign.
- Publish unverified color values as official brand colors.
- Stretch, skew, distort, or apply unapproved effects to brand artwork.
- Mix unrelated icon styles in the same interface.
- use unlicensed fonts, images, or illustrations.
- imply that a planned feature or product has already shipped.
- publish private internal contact details or confidential project information.

## 11. Brand asset management

Keep approved source assets in a clearly named repository location when the project owner has approved the files. Record:

- Asset name and intended use.
- Original source and owner.
- Approved variants and backgrounds.
- File format and dimensions.
- License or usage restrictions, where relevant.
- Approval status and revision date.

Do not overwrite an approved source asset with an unreviewed redesign. Preserve a recoverable original.

## 12. Review checklist

- [ ] Correct official company or product mark is used.
- [ ] Logo proportions and artwork are preserved.
- [ ] Colors are approved or clearly marked provisional.
- [ ] Typography is readable and appropriately licensed.
- [ ] Icons and illustrations have consistent style and permitted usage.
- [ ] Layout works at the intended screen sizes.
- [ ] Product naming and status claims are accurate.
- [ ] No private or sensitive information appears in published assets.

## 13. Maintenance

Update this guide when the project owner approves new logo artwork, exact color values, fonts, product marks, or shared interface standards. Until those decisions are documented, treat unspecified visual values as provisional rather than official.
